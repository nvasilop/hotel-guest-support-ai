About us

Small hotels, big calm

We started on Kythnos with one idea: a hotel should feel like an exhale. Today AegeanStay is a group of boutique seaside hotels on the islands and the mainland coast, each one quiet, personal and close to the water.

Our front desk is open 24/7, and our team supports guests in Greek and English. Families, pets up to 10 kg (in selected rooms) and guests who need accessible rooms are all welcome.
